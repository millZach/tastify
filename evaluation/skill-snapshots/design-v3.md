# Design

## Choose a direction

Inspect the existing interface and supplied references. Preserve established identity and behavior for scoped edits. For a new direction, inspect relevant rendered references and identify what the visitor should feel, understand, and do. Match the brief's visual ambition, including spectacle when requested.

Choose a composition and defining visual or interaction that fit the subject. Let typography, imagery, space, and behavior carry the expression. Explore alternatives only to resolve a consequential creative uncertainty; test that difference in a small rendered example before expanding it. Concentrate refinement on the focal experience.

## Edit the page

Give each section a clear purpose and heading where needed. Add supporting copy only when it contributes new information. For every caption, eyebrow, badge, and helper line, ask what the visitor learns or can do because it exists. Remove repeated taglines, annotations that add no meaning, and unnecessary section introductions. Keep useful control labels, units, instructions, and state feedback.

Start with 16–18px body text, at least 14px controls and meaningful labels, and at least 12px secondary text. Judge readability at normal zoom on desktop and phone, including contrast. Establish hierarchy through placement, spacing, weight, and scale. When space is tight, simplify or recompose the layout before shrinking text. Inspect text inside scaled SVGs and canvases at its actual displayed size.

## Make the visual work

Build the defining interaction early. Make the invitation, response, and result understandable on pointer, touch, and keyboard; provide a coherent reduced-motion state. Keep controls near the content they affect.

Fit animated artwork to both dimensions of its drawing area, allowing room for its full motion and interaction extremes. Inspect the silhouette through rotation, pointer movement, playback, and relevant transitions. Correct accidental cropping at the canvas, SVG, camera, or container that causes it. Use deliberate cropping only when it supports the composition.

## Review the rendered result

Inspect the complete page at desktop, phone, and an intermediate width, at normal zoom. For scoped edits, check affected components and states. Exercise the main journey and relevant failure states. Read every visible line: remove redundant text and fix small type, weak contrast, awkward wrapping, and competing emphasis. Check touch-target sizes.

Observe animated elements through a complete cycle or exercise their extreme states. Inspect their own drawing bounds as well as page overflow. Compare the focal experience with the brief and references: it should retain its intended impact as details are simplified.

Fix the largest visible or behavioral defect, then repeat the affected check. Finish with both working interactions and a clean visual review; report anything that could not be verified.

# Design solo

Do the design, implementation, and critique yourself; do not delegate. Preserve the user's constraints and supplied identity. For a scoped edit, work directly within the existing design.

## Explore before committing

Identify what the visitor should feel, understand, and do. Let that purpose set the visual ambition; an experience can make delight and spectacle its main purpose.

Inspect relevant rendered references to establish a craftsmanship benchmark. When the brief leaves creative choices open, make them yourself.

Build two small, runnable concepts in separate folders. Vary the composition and central visual idea. Each needs only the opening screen and the defining interaction, using the intended typography and key imagery. Reserve most of the available effort for completing and refining the selected concept.

## Select from what renders

Compare both concepts at matching desktop and phone sizes. Exercise the interaction. Judge these relationships:

- **Attention:** Identify what draws the eye first and next. Adjust competing scale, contrast, or placement until that order supports the experience.
- **Imagery:** Inspect the main visual at its displayed size. Judge silhouette, crop, edges, lighting, and material depth where relevant. Improve the central asset when it limits the whole composition.
- **Interaction:** Inspect the invitation, response, and resolution. The visitor's action should produce a perceptible, coherent result; preserve that relationship on touch and with reduced motion.
- **Identity:** Name the concrete visual decision that makes this fit the subject. Compare its execution with the references.

Choose one concept and state the visible reasons. If neither has a convincing central idea, rework the stronger candidate before expanding it.

## Develop and challenge

Carry the selected direction through the complete page. Compose each section around its content while retaining the identity. Keep the chosen opening as a visual reference during implementation.

Inspect the finished desktop and phone experience and exercise the main journey, including keyboard use. Apply the same comparison criteria and fix the largest visible weakness first. Reinspect the affected views and behavior. Complete the requested functionality and report any remaining verification gaps.

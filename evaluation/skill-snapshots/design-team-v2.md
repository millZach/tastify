# Design team

Preserve the user's constraints and supplied identity. For a scoped edit, work directly within the existing design. For a new direction, use the workflow below. If delegation is unavailable, disclose that limitation and perform the stages yourself.

## Explore before committing

Identify what the visitor should feel, understand, and do. Let that purpose set the visual ambition; an experience can make delight and spectacle its main purpose.

Spawn two independent concept builders with fresh contexts, separate folders, and separate preview ports. Give each the original brief, supplied references, project constraints, and the same visual criteria and exploration allowance. Each inspects relevant rendered references and chooses its own composition and central visual idea. Keep their work private from each other. Avoid prescribing your preferred aesthetic.

Builders implement only their assigned concept; critics report findings without editing. Neither delegates.

Each returns a runnable opening screen and defining interaction, with intended typography and key imagery, desktop and phone captures, and inspected references. Reserve most of the total effort for completing and refining the selected concept. While they explore, inspect integration constraints and prepare the main journey checks.

## Select from what renders

Give a fresh critic the original brief, references, and runnable candidates under neutral labels, without builder rationales or your preference. Have it inspect matching desktop and phone views and exercise the interactions. Require visible evidence using these criteria:

- **Attention:** Identify what draws the eye first and next. Adjust competing scale, contrast, or placement until that order supports the experience.
- **Imagery:** Inspect the main visual at its displayed size. Judge silhouette, crop, edges, lighting, and material depth where relevant. Improve the central asset when it limits the whole composition.
- **Interaction:** Inspect the invitation, response, and resolution. The visitor's action should produce a perceptible, coherent result; preserve that relationship on touch and with reduced motion.
- **Identity:** Name the concrete visual decision that makes this fit the subject. Compare its execution with the references.

The critic selects one concept with visible reasons or rejects both. Inspect the candidates yourself before choosing. If neither has a convincing central idea, have the stronger candidate reworked before expanding it.

## Develop and challenge

Develop the selected direction into the complete page. Compose each section around its content while retaining the identity. Keep the chosen opening as a visual reference during implementation.

Have a fresh critic inspect the finished desktop and phone experience and exercise the main journey, including keyboard use, using the same criteria. Fix the largest visible weakness first. Personally reinspect the affected views and behavior. Complete the requested functionality and report any remaining verification gaps, including substituted agent stages.
